Model scope
A transparent base case that can be stress-tested from the assumptions panel
Client assetsSeparate-account assets and securities-lending collateral are paired with equal liabilities. Forecast changes in these balances do not create company cash.
Cash and restricted cashCash flow ending cash includes restricted cash. The base case holds restricted cash at USD 22 million inside other assets.
Noncontrolling interestsCIPs and Subco earnings are assumed fully distributed in the year earned, leaving redeemable NCI balances flat.
Interest and debtInterest uses opening cash, investments and debt to avoid circular references. Debt issuance and repayments are editable.
Capital spendingPP&E rolls forward with capex less depreciation; intangible assets roll forward with amortization. No future acquisitions or impairments are assumed.
Historical basisFY2025 actuals include acquisition activity. Forecast years assume no acquisitions, no FX impact, no investment gains and no fund consolidation cash flows.
